---
title: "Basing 101: Rubble, Grass, and Snow"
date: 2026-06-05
excerpt: "Three basing styles to make any army feel finished."
---

A great base can do as much for a model as the paint job itself. Here are three approaches that work well for beginners and scale up nicely once you've got the basics down.

**Rubble** — broken bits of slate or resin scatter, painted in dry earth tones, works for almost any grimdark force.

**Grass tufts** — pre-made static grass tufts are the fastest way to make a fantasy army feel grounded in a real place.

**Snow** — a baking-soda-and-glue mix, drybrushed white, gives a convincing winter battlefield without needing specialty products.
