---
title: "Beginner's Guide: Picking Your First Army"
date: 2026-05-10
excerpt: "How to choose between factions when everything looks incredible."
---

The honest answer is: pick whichever models you'd be excited to paint, because that excitement is what gets an army finished. Rules and playstyle matter, but they're easy to adjust to — motivation is the part that's hard to fake.

If you're still stuck, come by on a Saturday and watch a few games. Seeing armies played is usually more useful than reading about them.
