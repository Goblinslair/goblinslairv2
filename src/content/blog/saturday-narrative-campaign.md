Every campaign starts the same way: a shared narrative premise, a map of contested objectives, and a group of players who agree to let the story matter as much as the win.

Lists are built once at the start and evolve slightly between rounds based on what happened in the last battle — losses have consequences, and big wins come with their own risks. It keeps games meaningful even outside a strict tournament format.

New round starts soon — ask at the counter if you'd like a seat at the table.
