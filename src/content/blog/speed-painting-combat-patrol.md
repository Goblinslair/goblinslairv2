Getting a new army to a "good enough for the table" standard doesn't have to eat your whole month. Here's the batch process we use in-store whenever a new Combat Patrol needs to be ready for Saturday.

**Day one** is all basecoats and washes — every model in the box gets primed and blocked in together, then a single wash pass ties the whole unit together in one go instead of model-by-model.

**Day two** is edge highlights and details, then a quick matte varnish. It won't win a painting competition, but it'll look great across the table and hold up to regular game nights.
